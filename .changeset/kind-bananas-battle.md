Adds support for [Azuracast](https://www.azuracast.com/) radio streams. Adds two default stations:

- [DjMuffinTops Radio](https://radio.djmuffintops.com/public/radio)
- [PMD Radio](https://radio.djmuffintops.com/public/pmd)

Guild administrators can add additional radio stations, choose the guild's default station, and remove stations that are no longer needed.
